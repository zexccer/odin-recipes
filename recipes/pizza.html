Pizza Dough

Easy to do at home pizza dough

This is a great recipe when you don’t want to wait for the dough to rise. You just mix it and allow it to rest for 5 minutes and then it’s ready to go! It yields a soft, chewy crust.

Ingredients

  • yeast [12g]
  • bread flour [850g]
  • water [550g about 65%]
  • salt [17g]
  • Olive oil or any type of oil

Steps

  1. Add flour and yeast, mix.
  2. Add water and mix until combined.
  3. Add salt and combine.
  4. It will be sticky but keep Kneading until you can stretch a piece and see through it without it breaking.
  5. Put in oiled bowl and let it rest for 2 hours. If you want better pizza, put in the fridge for 2-3 days.
  6. Shape into balls and place into a lightly floured baking sheet or proofing container.
  7. Let it proof for 1 hour if made the same day, or 1 - 2 hours if fridge cold. Until it is not quite doubled in size.
  8. Flour surface and dough. Spread and stretch.
  9. Put in pizza oven
